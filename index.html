About

The Biochemical Pathways wall charts were created by Gerhard Michal and published by Boehringer Mannheim, later Roche. After Roche stopped printing them, it put the posters online as an interactive tile map. This project's script downloaded those tiles and stitched the layers (background, enzymes, coenzymes, substrates, regulatory effects, higher plants, unicellular organisms) into a single printable image, with the coordinate grid removed.

The poster artwork belongs to Roche. This page only preserves the renders for personal and educational use.

The original download scripts are kept in legacy/ for reference. They no longer work.
